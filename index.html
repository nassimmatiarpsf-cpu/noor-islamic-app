Dua at the completion of the Qur'an

There is no single universally prescribed wording that every Muslim must use. You may make a sincere personal supplication asking Allah to accept your recitation, grant understanding, and help you live by the Qur'an.

اللَّهُمَّ تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا وَاجْعَلِ الْقُرْآنَ نُورًا لِقُلُوبِنَا
O Allah, accept from us, and make the Qur'an a light for our hearts. (A general supplication, not presented as a fixed prophetic wording.)

About Noor

Noor is currently a browser-based companion app. It uses an external service for Qur'an text and prayer-time calculations. It is not yet a native iOS App Store application, and it does not send your saved bookmarks or counters to a server.

For production release, the Qur'an data and translations should be verified, privacy and accessibility reviewed, prayer notifications implemented with a reliable delivery mechanism, and religious calculations reviewed by qualified people.
